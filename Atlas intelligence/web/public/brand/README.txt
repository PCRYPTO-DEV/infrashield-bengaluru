USAGE
Keep clear space around the logo at least equal to 1/4 of symbol height.
Use the full lockup above 240 px wide. Below that, use the symbol only.
Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, add shadows, or place on visually noisy backgrounds.

NOTE
The living-city artwork originates from the selected Concept 01 visual and is supplied as transparent production PNGs plus portable SVG wrappers. For trademark filing or very large-format fabrication, commission a final manually traced vector master from this approved form.
