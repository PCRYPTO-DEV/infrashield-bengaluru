## Product
- Product name: Polymath InfraShield Bengaluru. Brand: ATBOSE / Polymath. Status: MVP v1.0.
- It is a decision-support platform for critical-infrastructure continuity planning in Bengaluru.
- It scores every mapped facility with a Continuity Risk Score (CRS) from 0 to 100.
- Risk levels: Low / Medium / High / Critical / Insufficient Data.
- CRS dimensions: power dependency and backup-readiness gap, flood and drainage exposure,
  water dependency, road and emergency-access risk, heat exposure, data confidence.
- Facility types covered: hospitals, water utilities, power substations, tech parks,
  emergency services, transport hubs.
- Outputs: interactive facility map, facility risk cards with driver analysis,
  top-10 most vulnerable facilities table, zone-level observations, missing-data
  checklist, executive mitigation report, CSV and HTML export.
- Geography: Bengaluru Urban District. Demo zones include Electronic City, Whitefield,
  Bellandur, Koramangala, Hebbal, Yelahanka, KR Puram, Peenya, Central Bengaluru,
  Sarjapur Road, Manyata/Nagavara, Marathahalli, Indiranagar, HSR Layout, Majestic.
- MVP data: public facility names and approximate coordinates, synthetic/estimated
  risk scores, open media references for flood zones. No SCADA, no patient data.
- It is NOT a smart-city dashboard, SCADA system, grid-control system, or
  real-time monitoring system.
